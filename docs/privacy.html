Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This app is a personal tool used by its owner to upload the owner's own original videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

Information we access

When the owner authorizes the app through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (such as display name) for the owner's account only.

How information is used

The access token is used solely to upload videos to the owner's TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. We do not sell, share or disclose any information to third parties. We do not collect information from any other users.

Data deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services, and can delete locally stored tokens at any time.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the app owner through the contact information on the owner's TikTok profile.
